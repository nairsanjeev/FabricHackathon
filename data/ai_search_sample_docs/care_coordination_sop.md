# Care Coordination SOP

Document Type: SOP
Facility Scope: Community Medical Center
Effective Date: 2026-02-12

## Objective

Lower avoidable readmissions for high-risk chronic condition patients.

## Eligible Patients

- Two or more inpatient admissions in 6 months
- Three or more chronic conditions
- No PCP visit in previous 12 months

## Required Steps

1. Assign nurse care manager before discharge.
2. Complete social risk screening.
3. Schedule PCP visit within 7 days.
4. Confirm transportation and pharmacy access.
5. Document outreach completion in care coordination log.
