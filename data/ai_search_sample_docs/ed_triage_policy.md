# Emergency Department Triage Policy

Document Type: Triage Policy
Facility Scope: Metro General Hospital ED
Effective Date: 2026-01-10

## Priority Levels

- Level 1: Immediate life threat, immediate bed placement.
- Level 2: High risk or severe pain, provider in <= 10 minutes.
- Level 3: Stable, diagnostics likely required.
- Level 4: Stable, one resource expected.
- Level 5: Stable, no resources expected.

## Throughput Targets

- Door-to-triage <= 10 minutes
- Door-to-provider <= 30 minutes for Level 2 and Level 3

## Frequent Flyer Flag

Patients with 4 or more ED visits in 12 months should trigger:

1. Care coordination referral
2. Social determinants screening
3. PCP follow-up scheduling before discharge
