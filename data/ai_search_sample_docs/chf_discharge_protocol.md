# CHF Discharge Protocol - HealthFirst

Document Type: Discharge Protocol
Facility Scope: Metro General Hospital, Community Medical Center, Riverside Health Center
Effective Date: 2026-01-15

## Purpose

Reduce 30-day readmissions for patients discharged with congestive heart failure (CHF).

## Required Actions Before Discharge

1. Complete medication reconciliation and confirm diuretic plan.
2. Provide written low-sodium diet education.
3. Confirm daily weight monitoring instructions.
4. Schedule cardiology or primary care follow-up within 7 days.
5. Document teach-back completion in discharge note.

## High-Risk Criteria

- Prior CHF admission within 30 days
- Chronic kidney disease stage 3 or higher
- Lives alone with limited caregiver support
- More than 8 active medications

## Post-Discharge Follow-up

- Phone outreach within 48 hours
- Medication adherence check
- Escalate if dyspnea, edema, or weight gain >2 pounds in 24 hours
