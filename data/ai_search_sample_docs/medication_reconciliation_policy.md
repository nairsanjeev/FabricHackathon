# Medication Reconciliation Policy

Document Type: Medication Policy
Facility Scope: Riverside Health Center
Effective Date: 2026-01-05

## Scope

Applies to all inpatient admissions, transfers, and discharges.

## Required Process

1. Compare home medications with admission orders.
2. Resolve duplicate therapies and interactions.
3. Verify allergies and contraindications.
4. Provide updated medication list at discharge.
5. Counsel patient and caregiver using teach-back.

## Quality Measure

Medication reconciliation completion rate target is >= 95 percent for all discharges.
